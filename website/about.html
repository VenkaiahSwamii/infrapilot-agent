Philosophy

I believe in the power of simplicity. The best systems are those that solve complex problems with elegant, understandable solutions. I strive to:

  • Write code that humans can read — because code is read far more often than it's written
  • Design for the long term — building systems that can evolve and scale with changing requirements
  • Embrace pragmatism — choosing the right tool for the job, not the trendiest one
  • Document everything — because knowledge silos are the biggest bottleneck in engineering teams
  • Ship early, iterate often — because perfect is the enemy of good
